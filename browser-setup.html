CINEPRESENCE / BROWSER COMPANION

More details.
Better detection.

Your app’s local browser connection is prepared. Enable the optional companion to read movie and episode metadata from streaming pages, even when their tab titles are vague.

1. Open your extensions page

In Edge, paste edge://extensions into the address bar.
In Chrome, use chrome://extensions.

Turn on Developer mode. This preview companion is not listed in the browser stores yet.

2. Select “Load unpacked”

Choose this installed folder:

browser-companion (beside CinePresence.exe)

The browser asks for access to pages so the companion can read headings, embedded movie/episode metadata and video timing. It does not read passwords, cookies, messages or browsing history. It sends no page data to a hosted service.

3. Reload your streaming tab and play

Keep CinePresence and Discord open. Pin the companion’s toolbar button to check its connection. If the page has no useful title at all, enter a movie/show name there; use Correct match in the app to choose the exact TMDB result.

Spotify, Facebook, YouTube, Twitter/X, Instagram, VK, Vimeo, Dailymotion and the other excluded platforms remain blocked, including their embedded players. The companion skips private windows. Websites that expose no readable video or identifying metadata may still need manual help.

The installer prepares the native connection automatically. Browsers do not allow ordinary Windows installers to silently enable an unlisted extension. No browser policies or security settings are changed. You can remove the companion from your extensions page at any time.
